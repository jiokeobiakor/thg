Privacy Policy

Who we are: The Hair Gallery, a hair booking business. Contact us by Instagram DM: @thehairgallery_thg.

What we collect: your name, phone number, email, the hairstyle you book, your appointment date and time, and any notes you type.

Why: only to manage your appointment and contact you about it.

Who can see it: the owner of The Hair Gallery. Our website hosting (Vercel) and database (Supabase) providers store it on our behalf. We do not sell your data.

How long: until you delete your account or ask us to delete it.

Your rights: you can see your bookings in "My Bookings", ask us to correct your details, or delete your account and all your data with the "Delete my account" link there, or by messaging us.
