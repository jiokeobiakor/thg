## 2. Pick a good target (all 4 boxes)
- [ ] Business account with 500–20,000 followers
- [ ] Posted in the last 2 weeks
- [ ] Bio or posts say "DM to book", "DM to order" or "send a DM for price"
- [ ] No website link in bio, or only a Linktree/WhatsApp link

Skip it if: the account is personal, it already has a proper website, or it posted nothing recently.

## 3. What to send

**Instagram (services: hair, beauty, barber, tailor, photographer)**
> Hi [name]! Your [work] is clean 🔥 Do you still take bookings only through DMs? I build websites where clients see your prices and book a date and time themselves, and you get a dashboard with your revenue. Here's one running a real business: [demo link]. Want a free mock-up with your name and logo?

**Instagram (goods: food, vendors, boutiques)**
> Hi [name]! Your [cakes/products] look amazing 😍 Quick one: do customers still order only in your DMs? I build simple shop websites where customers order online, stock updates on its own, and you see your sales and best sellers each month. Demo: [demo link]. Can I make you a free mock-up?

**LinkedIn (connection note, max 300 characters)**
> Hi [name], I'm an MIS student who builds websites for small businesses: online bookings, orders and a revenue dashboard. One already runs a real salon. Would love to connect.

**LinkedIn (after they accept)**
> Thanks for connecting, [name]! I noticed [business] takes orders through DMs/WhatsApp. I set up websites where customers book or order online and the owner sees revenue and trends in one place. Here's a live example: [demo link]. Would a free mock-up with your branding be useful?

**WhatsApp / Facebook groups (after you've been helpful there for a week)**
> Hi everyone 👋🏾 I build websites for small businesses: customers see your prices and book or order online, and you get a dashboard of your sales. Here's one running a real business: [demo link]. DM me if you want a free mock-up with your name.

**Follow-up (3 days later, only once)**
> Hi [name], just checking you saw the demo. Happy to answer any questions 🙏

**When they ask "how much?"**
> Starter is ₦120k one-off + ₦10k/month for support (Pro with shop and email alerts: ₦220k + ₦15k/month). You pay half to start and half when it's live. I'll send you a free mock-up first so you can see it.

## 5. Rules
- Message business accounts only. Never message the same person more than twice. Stop when someone says no.
- Send messages yourself, by hand. No bots or bulk-DM apps: they get your account banned.
- Change a few words each time and mention something real from their page, so it doesn't look like spam.
